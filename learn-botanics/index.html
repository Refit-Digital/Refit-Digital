Why herbal medicine?

Rooted in tradition.

Welcome

Hello, I'm Claire

I'm an experienced Western Medical Herbalist (BSc), a member of the National Institute of Medical Herbalists (NIMH) and a member of the Federation of Holistic Therapists (FHT).

I've worked as a Massage Therapist and Aromatherapist since 2010 and am trained in Aromatherapy, Indian Head Massage, Reflexology and Holistic Massage. With a strong background in facilitation as a Forest School Leader, my approach is deeply rooted in nature connection.

Whilst I see a range of patients as part of my general practice, my background in education and bodywork has led to a special interest in hormonal health, specialising in female gynaecology and reproductive health. Common issues I help solve are hormonal imbalances, skin complaints, mental health, stress, digestion complaints and sleep issues. I believe passionately in the power of menstrual cycle awareness, having trained with Red School as a Menstruality Mentor.

I have used my 14 plus years of experience working in clinical settings to craft my sessions to make my patients feel warm, listened to and empowered in treatment. I use a range of biomedical tools to assess my patients, calling on my orthodox medical training as well as traditional medicine disciplines, curating holistic treatment protocols to help bring the body back into balance.

I also have a strong passion for young people's health and wellbeing. I deliver a range of empowering educational programmes as part of my practice; helping people develop their own therapeutic connection with nature, offering accessible ways to bring herbs into one's daily life through diet and seasonal foraging tips.

Frequently asked questions

There are many factors when considering whether herbal medicine is the best route to better health and wellbeing. Here are some of the questions I'm asked most.

A Western modern herbalism consultation involves an in-depth assessment of your current health condition and presenting symptoms, including taking blood pressure and other clinical diagnostic tests where appropriate. The consultation will go through your and your family's medical history, medication, plus lifestyle factors such as diet and sleep pattern.

Although Medical Herbalists have received in-depth medical training over a 3–5 year science degree or equivalent qualification, we cannot legally diagnose health conditions.

We may write to your GP if necessary and/or will advise you to make a GP appointment if there is any cause for concern. We often work together as a multi-disciplinary team so we can best support you.

We are all very different, so unfortunately there is not just one definitive answer to this question. Broadly speaking, treatment may vary anywhere between 1 and 12 months, depending on whether the condition is acute (first aid, common colds, sprains, allergy) or chronic (auto-immune, gynaecological conditions, heart health, diabetes).

Yes — under supervision from a herbalist, it is safe to take herbal medicine with your medication. In some cases, herbal medicine may be able to help decrease the dosage of your medication, with consent from your GP and close monitoring from your herbalist. There are some herbs that have been known to interact with pharmaceutical drugs, so depending on the medication, those will be avoided.

Depending on the condition, I will track your symptoms over the course of 2–3 months. For example, for endometriosis, I will want to know your symptoms at each stage of your menstrual cycle (there are four stages). This helps me understand how mood, pain levels, cravings and energy levels differ at each stage, and lets me work out how best to support your health for the long term.

Another example is treating menopausal symptoms such as anxiety, brain fog, low energy and hot flushes. I will want to monitor your symptoms over 1–3 months to really get to the root cause.
